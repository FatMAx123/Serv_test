Inside a 24-meter sunken crater nestled between grassy green hills, a massive 1.5-meter riveted iron steam pipeline has fractured open, violently blasting towering geysers of boiling white steam and hissing vapor into the sky. Concept art of the Eastern Basalt Pass and Valve Control Station in Astard Hills (Project Steam, Lineage 2 and steampunk fantasy style). A narrow rocky canyon pass between steep green hillside bluffs with dark volcanic basalt scree. Built into the cliff face is a heavy cast-iron steam manifold and pumping valve station, featuring oversized bronze handwheels, pressure pipes, and exposed brass gearboxes. On the stony path, aggressive steampunk automata patrol: a bipedal square hydraulic stamping robot (Холмовой Трамбовщик / Hill Presser) with heavy crushing pistons, and a quadruped brass hound with glowing vents (Паровая Гончая / Steam Hound).
